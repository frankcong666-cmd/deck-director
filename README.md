# Deck Director

**Deck Director** is a reusable, end-to-end workflow for creating compelling presentations.

It directs the entire process, from a rough idea or source material to a polished final deck: narrative, visual direction, assets, slide production, critique, and delivery.

## What it does

Deck Director helps you:

- Clarify the audience, objective, speaking time, and key message
- Build a clear slide-by-slide narrative before designing pages
- Draft a natural spoken script and estimate timing
- Explore visual directions before committing to a style
- Adapt to your preferred style: minimal, cinematic, editorial, technical, warm, premium, playful, or something entirely your own
- Use real screenshots, data, and source material where evidence matters
- Use generated visuals for atmosphere, metaphor, and conceptual storytelling
- Produce an editable presentation and inspect it for hierarchy, alignment, readability, crop, contrast, and consistency
- Iterate precisely based on feedback without redesigning approved work

## The workflow

1. **Brief** — Define the audience, goal, constraints, references, and visual preferences.
2. **Story** — Form one central idea and a coherent slide-by-slide narrative.
3. **Art direction** — Choose composition, typography, color, imagery, and pacing.
4. **Evidence and assets** — Match each point with the most credible visual form.
5. **Production** — Build an editable deck with one clear job per slide.
6. **Critique and revision** — Render, inspect, diagnose, and improve the deck.
7. **Delivery** — Export the final deck and supporting materials requested.
